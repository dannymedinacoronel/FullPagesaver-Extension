Everything you need to really save a web page

Not a screenshot: a complete, tidy copy ready to archive, read offline or print.

Self-contained HTML

A single .html file with images, styles and fonts embedded. Includes iframe content and saves each canvas as an image.

PDF with selectable text

Scrolls through the page, stitches the captures without gaps or repeated bars and adds an invisible text layer: copy and search with Ctrl+F.

Preview before saving

Tick or untick sections and parts of the page (header, menus, footer, comments, videos…) and see how the document will look.

Clean and your way

Remove ads, cookie notices and floating buttons. Reading mode, highlight, delete paragraphs, add notes, cover page and index.

Several tabs at once

One HTML or one PDF per tab, or all of them in a single PDF. Pick the ones you want from the list.

HTML to PDF converter

Convert already-saved HTML files with no file limit: one by one or a whole folder, as one PDF per file or a combined one.

Smart with tricky sites

PDF viewers, apps like Gmail or Drive (app mode), capture speed and automatic re-check of anything that didn't load.

Private by design

Everything is processed on your computer. No accounts, no servers, no analytics and no remote code.

Three simple steps

  1. 1

    Open the page

    Go to the website you want to save and click the FullPage Saver icon (or Alt+Shift+P for an instant PDF).

  2. 2

    Choose and review

    Pick HTML, PDF or MHT. In the preview, remove what you don't need: menus, ads, comments, cookie notices…

  3. 3

    Save

    Chrome lets you choose where to save the file. Ready to open offline, print or archive.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, FullPage Saver is completely free, with no sign-up or artificial limits.

Does it send my pages to a server?

No. Everything is processed in your browser and saved to your computer through the download you confirm. There are no servers of its own and no analytics.

What permissions does it ask for, and why?

activeTab and scripting to read and scroll the tab when you press a button, downloads to save the file and pageCapture for the .mht format. Access to all sites is optional and only requested with a button, to embed resources from other domains.

The PDF has blank parts or missing pages

Set the capture speed to “Slow” and don't switch tabs while it captures. The automatic re-check captures again anything that hadn't finished loading.

Does it work with Gmail, Drive or PDF viewers?

Yes: app mode scrolls only the main panel of apps like Gmail, Drive or Outlook, and in PDF viewers it copies each page at its original size with its text.

Which languages is it available in?

Español, català, English, français, português, Deutsch and italiano. You choose the language the first time you open it.
